## Overview

This privacy policy explains how koborin.ai ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website.

## Data We Collect

### Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors interact with our website. This service collects:

- Pages you visit and time spent on each page
- Your approximate geographic location (country/city level)
- Device and browser information
- Referral source (how you found our site)

This data is collected using pseudonymous identifiers, not your name or contact details, and is used only to improve our content and user experience. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information. You can opt out of analytics tracking by declining cookies when prompted.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Comments (Giscus)

Our comment system is powered by [Giscus](https://giscus.app/), which uses GitHub Discussions. When you leave a comment:

- You authenticate through your GitHub account
- Your GitHub username and avatar are displayed with your comment
- Comments are stored in our GitHub repository's Discussions

When the comment section on an article loads, content is fetched from Giscus (giscus.app) and GitHub, which receive data such as your IP address and the page URL. If you sign in with GitHub, a session token is stored in your browser's local storage (`giscus-session`).

We do not have access to your GitHub password. For more information, see [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `cookie_consent` (local storage) | Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics | Kept until you clear it |
| `starlight-theme` (local storage) | Light/dark theme preference | Kept until you clear it |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics tracking (only with consent) | 2 years |

To change or withdraw consent, use "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page. If you decline, existing Google Analytics cookies (such as `_ga`) are deleted.

## Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to data processing
- Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

- **Google Analytics** - Web analytics
- **Giscus** - Comment system (via GitHub)
- **GitHub Sponsors** - Sponsor button on the About page (loaded from github.com; GitHub may set its own cookies)
- **Cloudflare** - Hosting and content delivery. Cloudflare processes request data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site.

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

## Data Retention

- Google Analytics keeps event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months (the user-level period restarts when you visit again).
- Comments remain until deleted by the author or repository owner
- Cookie consent preferences are stored locally in your browser
